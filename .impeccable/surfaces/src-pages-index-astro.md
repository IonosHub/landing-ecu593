# Surface: landing (src/pages/index.astro)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Ecuadorian families (kids/teens) and adults choosing an English course, mostly on mobile. Action: submit the lead form (POST /v1/leads/register) or open WhatsApp. Proof: the 12-level × 4-week path, 7/10 pass mark, written + oral evaluation, family accounts with visible progress. Constraints: no invented testimonials, numbers, prices; pending data rendered via the pending() pattern and listed in PENDIENTES.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Learning English is a journey with a passport; each passed level is a stamp. Refuses the category default of a smiling-students photo hero, blue/white card grid and testimonial carousel.

OWN-WORLD: Ecuadorian passport. Night-navy cover (#14204A) for the hero spread and the closing section; mint security paper (#E6F0EC) with fine guilloché line patterns in tricolor ink (yellow #F2C230, blue #2E6FD8, red #D8352A) as the page ground. Rubber stamps (circles, rectangles, dated, slightly rotated, ink-bleed edges) are the primary graphic and state marker. Monospaced MRZ lines and ruled data fields (label above value, small caps) for every fact. Photos sit in die-cut photo windows with a guilloché frame. Buttons are inked stamps/stamped tickets. No plain white sections.

STORY: The visitor sees "curso de inglés" immediately, recognises their age program (Kids / Teens / Adultos visas), watches the 12 levels get stamped as they scroll, sees in-person and online entry stamps, and fills the "solicitud" form or opens WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: an open passport spread fills the viewport on the navy cover. Left page is the data page: logo in the photo window, fields "Titular: Tú", "Destino: Inglés", "Niveles: 12 × 4 semanas", "Modalidad: Presencial · Online", and the MRZ strip P<ECU593<<LEARN<<GROW<<ACHIEVE at the bottom. Right page holds the eyebrow "Ecu593 English · Curso de inglés", a large display headline, a subline, the primary stamp-button "Quiero inscribirme" and a secondary WhatsApp button. Three stamps thump on with staggered timing. Mobile: the spread stacks with the right page first and the data page below.

FORM: Pasaporte de 12 sellos, position 3 of my ordered grounded list (assigned by the roll), seed key 0d81d484. Raises: ruled label discipline (sneaker archive), die-cut photo windows (toy window-box), total ground commitment (mezzotint). Signature interaction: the 12-level stamp path, where each level card is stamped as it enters view and a counter shows "Nivel n/12 · semanas". Motion grammar: stamp thump (scale 1.4→1, rotate settle, ink opacity) and page-turn reveals; everything is static under reduced motion.
